# Product Management Workflow

This workflow explains how to add products in the CMS and generate optimized static pages for them.

## 1. Add Product in CMS

1.  Navigate to the Admin Dashboard: `http://localhost:1313/admin/` (or your local URL).
1.  Hugo should automatically detect the new files and rebuild.
2.  Visit `http://localhost:1313/products/<slug>/` to see the new product page.
